# ARTAFIC — About Page Design System

## 1. Hero / Intro
- **Pattern:** Editorial Opening
- **Composition:** Asymmetric typography block. 
  - Top-left: Small label "ABOUT / ARTAFIC"
  - Line 1: "We build" (Medium)
  - Line 2: "digital experiences" (Large Editorial Display - Cormorant Garamond)
  - Line 3: "with intention." (Smaller, offset)
- **Visual:** Abstract digital-art composition (SVG geometry, thin lines, grid) sitting partially behind/next to the typography.
- **Motion:** Subtle text mask reveal + slight vertical movement.

## 2. Why ARTAFIC Exists
- **Pattern:** Wide Editorial Composition
- **Composition:** 
  - Left 60%: Large statement ("A good business can lose attention...")
  - Right 30-35%: Supporting paragraph
- **Visual:** Horizontal rule connecting the two areas.
- **Sub-sections:** 01 THE OBSERVATION, 02 THE DISCONNECT, 03 THE SOLUTION
  - NOT CARDS. Three large editorial blocks spanning the page, connected by a line that progresses on scroll.

## 3. Our Story / Transition
- **Pattern:** Horizontal Editorial Timeline
- **Composition:** 01 OBSERVE, 02 CONNECT, 03 BUILD (Horizontal on desktop, vertical on mobile).
- **Motion:** Scroll-driven typography transition, subtle active state.

## 4. Philosophy
- **Pattern:** Sticky Editorial Scroll
- **Composition:** 
  - Left: Sticky large typography.
  - Right: Three principles (01 DESIGN WITH PURPOSE, 02 BUILD WITH INTENTION, 03 MAKE IT DISTINCTIVE).
- **Motion:** Active principle becomes prominent on scroll (opacity, slight scale, accent line).

## 5. LESS NOISE. MORE IDENTITY.
- **Pattern:** Preserved Signature Section
- **Composition:** "LESS NOISE." (large) and "MORE IDENTITY." (larger), positioned differently.
- **Visuals:** Strong editorial font, subtle parallax, Floating Paths background, grain, thin rules.
- **Motion:** Slow, cinematic motion (preserved).

## 6. What We Believe
- **Pattern:** Editorial Manifesto
- **Composition:** Asymmetric positioning in space (CLEAR upper left, PURPOSE center/right, etc.), aligned to an underlying grid.
- **Motion:** Active statement becomes sharper/brighter on scroll, others muted.

## 7. ARTAFIC Mindset
- **Pattern:** Large Editorial Quotation
- **Composition:** Dominant visual quotation. Narrow supporting column. Technical label "SEC-07 MINDSET_SYS".
- **Visuals:** Micro labels, thin borders, tiny grid, small coordinates.

## 8. Final Statement & CTA
- **Pattern:** Quiet Ending + Minimal Editorial CTA
- **Composition:** Huge negative space. Quiet opacity reveal.
- **CTA:** "HAVE SOMETHING WORTH BUILDING?" + "LET'S TALK"
- **Motion:** Magnetic button interaction or subtle directional hover. No generic rounded CTA cards.
