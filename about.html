01 / IDENTITY

Who We Are

Websites • Logos • Clean Code

EST. 2024 • CRAFT STUDIO

ARTAFIC is an independent digital studio. We build custom websites and design clean logos for businesses that take themselves seriously.

We don't use pre-made templates, we don't install heavy page builders that slow your site down, and we don't hide behind agency buzzwords. You work directly with the designer and developer building your project from start to finish.

“A website has one job: make your business look credible, explain what you do clearly, and turn visitors into customers.”

02 / THE PURPOSE

Good businesses deserve to look the part online.

You can run a great business, but if your website looks outdated or broken on mobile, people will judge you by your screen. We exist because good businesses deserve to look just as good online as they are in real life.

+ + THE PROBLEM

What holds most websites back:

  • Generic templates that make you look like every other competitor
  • Heavy codebases that take five seconds to load on a phone
  • Confusing navigation and buzzwords that make visitors leave immediately
+ + OUR APPROACH

How we build your site:

  • Custom design shaped around your actual business and services
  • Clean, lightweight code that opens instantly on any device
  • Simple structure that makes your offer easy to understand and trust
