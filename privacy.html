Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

ARTAFIC is a digital agency website. We do not require account registration, customer logins, or payment processing on this website. The only personal information we collect is information you voluntarily submit to us through our project contact form:

  • Your Name
  • Email Address
  • Service of Interest (Web Development or Logo Building)
  • Project Details provided in your message

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit strictly to evaluate your project request, respond to your inquiry, and communicate with you regarding potential agency work. We do not sell, rent, or distribute your contact details to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

This website does not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or third-party behavioral profiling trackers. We do not track your activity across third-party websites.

4. Third-Party Hosting & Infrastructure

Our website is hosted on static cloud infrastructure (such as Vercel) and uses public CDN services for web fonts and static libraries. These network providers may process standard HTTP server request logs (including IP addresses) solely for operational security and traffic delivery.
