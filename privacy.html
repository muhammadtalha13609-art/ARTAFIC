PRIVACY POLICY

How we handle your information with total transparency, minimal footprint, and zero third-party tracking.

DATA

01 / COLLECTION

Information We Collect

Voluntary Submission • Minimal Footprint

ZERO LOGINS • STRICTLY DIRECT

ARTAFIC is a digital craft agency website. We do not require account registration, passwords, customer logins, or credit card processing on this website.

The only personal information we collect is information you voluntarily submit to us through our project contact form or direct email inquiry. We gather only what is strictly necessary to review your project and get back to you with an honest proposal:

+ + 01 / IDENTITY

Your Name

Used to address you personally in our direct correspondence and project consultations.

+ + 02 / CONTACT

Email Address

Used strictly to respond to your inquiry, provide project estimates, and coordinate deliverables.

+ + 03 / SCOPE

Service of Interest

Web Development, Logo Building, or bespoke digital direction to assign the right specialist.

+ + 04 / CONTEXT

Project Details

Any background context, timeline, or design references provided voluntarily in your message.

We never collect sensitive personal identification, financial credentials, or background telemetry. All project contracts and transactions occur through separate, mutually verified business channels.

SECURITY

02 / USAGE & INFRASTRUCTURE

How We Use & Protect Your Information

We treat your project data with the same discretion we bring to our code. No ad networks, no data brokers, and no algorithmic profiling.

+ + DIRECT PROJECT USE

Evaluating & Building Your Project

We use the information you submit strictly to evaluate your project request, respond to your inquiry, and communicate with you regarding potential agency work.

  • Direct communication with the designers and developers crafting your site.
  • Preparation of transparent project scopes, timelines, and architectural roadmaps.
  • Zero marketing spam, automated mass-blast email campaigns, or cold calling.
  • We never sell, rent, or distribute your contact details to third-party advertisers or data brokers.
+ + INFRASTRUCTURE & HOSTING

Secure Cloud Delivery

Our website is hosted on static cloud infrastructure (such as Vercel) and uses public CDN services for web fonts and static libraries.

  • Modern HTTPS encryption safeguarding all data transfers across every page view.
  • Network providers process standard HTTP server request logs (including IP addresses) solely for operational security and traffic delivery.
  • Zero exposed relational database vulnerabilities or user storage backdoors.
  • Rigorous Content Security Policy isolating external dependencies and scripts.
