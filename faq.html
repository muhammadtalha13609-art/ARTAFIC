ARTAFIC is a digital agency that helps businesses improve their online presence through professionally designed and developed websites and logos. We focus on two services: Web Development and Logo Building. Our goal is to ensure that the quality of your website reflects the quality of your actual business.

ARTAFIC currently offers two services: Web Development — professional, custom, responsive websites built around your business goals and customers — and Logo Building — professional logo design for businesses that need a stronger, clearer visual identity. We deliberately keep our focus narrow to ensure quality in both areas.

Yes. Redesigning an existing website is one of the most common project types we work on. If your current site is outdated, underperforming, or no longer represents your business accurately, we can work with you to redesign it from the ground up — keeping what works and replacing what doesn't.

Our process has four stages: Understand (we learn about the business, audience, and goals), Design (we create a clear visual and UX direction), Build (we develop the responsive website from the approved design), and Refine (we test, review, and polish before delivery). Each stage involves your review and approval so there are no surprises.

Timeline depends on the scope and complexity of the project. Every project is different. The best way to get an accurate timeline is to get in touch where we can understand your specific requirements and give you a realistic estimate based on your goals and the work involved.

Project pricing is based on scope, requirements, complexity, and business goals. We don't publish fixed packages because no two projects are the same. The right approach is a conversation — get in touch and we can discuss your project and provide a proper estimate.
